# Falco 2.6.1 — Faster refinement

## Optional acceleration offer

When Phonon is installed and refinement, voice editing or Command Mode is active,
Rune's Overview and Settings can show **Try faster refinement?**. It offers the
matching **215 MB DFlash2 drafter** and explains the hardware-dependent benefit.

- **Download & enable** starts a cancellable download with progress in the same
  window. Only a successful download enables acceleration. Restart dictation
  when ready to load it; the offer never restarts an ongoing recording.
- **Not now**, Esc and Close remember your choice without downloading. You can
  enable acceleration later in **Settings → Refiner**. Failed or cancelled
  attempts keep it off and can be retried there or after reopening Rune.
- The offer waits during recordings, background operations and unsaved settings.
  It stays inside Rune; it does not open a window over another application.
- Gemma, Llama and setups with no active AI feature do not receive it. Existing
  acceleration choices are retained. The GTK compatibility preferences retain
  their manual acceleration control.

The initial 2.5 Linux benchmark measured **32% less total refiner-request time**
on an RTX 4060 Laptop GPU across four synthetic prompts. This patch retains that
engine; it does not introduce a new speed measurement. CPU-only and very long
outputs remain unmeasured, and acceleration can be slower on some devices.
[Benchmark method and raw data](../benchmarks/README.md).

## Download repair

Rune's background worker lacked the `wait()` method required by the downloader's
cancellation interface. This caused a failure before fetching even an already
cached model, displayed as “The download did not finish. Check your connection
and retry.” The token now supports interruptible retry waits. Model downloads
work again, keep checksum verification, and report the actual failure reason
when one is available. The stale “Working…” notice is cleared when a background
job takes over status reporting.

## Parakeet acceleration research

No published Parakeet Ultra speculative drafter and compatible verifier was
found for Falco's shipped INT8 ONNX or Vulkan GGML backends. Phonon's DFlash2
model accelerates text refinement only. Photon, CUDA/TensorRT and native NeMo
runtimes warrant separate evaluation; no Falco speed gain is claimed or enabled
for them in this patch. [Primary sources and compatibility findings](../research/parakeet-acceleration-2.6.1.md).

## Upgrade

```sh
curl -fsSL https://falco.lothnic.dev/install.sh | bash
```

Quit Falco and close existing windows before reopening after an upgrade.
Settings, vocabulary and downloaded models are kept. The verified 0.1.0-only
repair from 2.6.0 remains in the installer; newer releases use the normal update
path. Package-managed copies update through their package manager.
